pKL versus KL: growth data

Figures and processed summaries for the computations accompanying Almost all pKL basis elements are bad. The plots compare the p-canonical and Kazhdan--Lusztig bases in available finite types, mostly at p = 2 and p = 3.

Raw Magma output is in data/raw; processed CSV summaries are in data/processed; the Magma summary scripts are in scripts. Click a figure for the high-resolution PNG. PDF versions are stored next to the PNGs.
